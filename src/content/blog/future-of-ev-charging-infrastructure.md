Range anxiety was always really a charging problem. Solve where and how quickly
a car can be topped up, and the range figure stops mattering nearly as much.

## Charging where the car already is

Most charging happens where a car sits for hours anyway — at home, at work, in
a car park. The network that matters is the unglamorous one.

## The long journey case

Fast charging on trunk routes fills the remaining gap. Thirty minutes to eighty
per cent turns a barrier into a coffee stop.
