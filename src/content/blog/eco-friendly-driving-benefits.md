The environmental case for an electric car is stronger than it first looks,
because it improves every year the grid does. A petrol car is as clean on its
last day as its first.

## Efficiency, not just emissions

An electric drivetrain turns most of the energy it is given into motion. That
efficiency is why the comparison holds even where the electricity is not
especially clean.

## The whole life of the car

Manufacturing a battery has a cost. It is repaid within the first years of
driving, and the pack itself has a second life storing energy long after it
leaves the car.
