Cities were built around a car that no longer exists. Narrower, quieter and
better suited to short journeys, the electric city car is quietly changing what
a street needs to be.

## Less space, better used

A smaller footprint means more of the street can go back to the people using
it. Compact electric vehicles park in half the space and manoeuvre through
traffic that would defeat something larger.

## Cleaner where it matters most

Tailpipe emissions do their worst in exactly the places people gather. Removing
them from dense neighbourhoods is the single clearest air-quality win available
to most cities.
