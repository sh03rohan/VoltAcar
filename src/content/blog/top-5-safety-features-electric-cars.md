An electric platform is a chance to rethink the structure around the people
inside it. No engine block at the front means more room to design a crumple
zone that behaves the way you want it to.

## A lower centre of gravity

A floor-mounted battery sits low and spreads its weight evenly, which makes a
rollover far less likely and a sudden avoidance manoeuvre far more composed.

## Systems that watch the road

Lane keeping, blind-spot monitoring and automatic emergency braking have moved
from options list to standard fit, and they intervene most usefully in exactly
the situations drivers misjudge.
